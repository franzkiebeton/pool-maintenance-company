FAQ

Pool service questions, answered

How much does weekly pool service cost?

Most pools fall into one flat monthly rate based on size and features like a spa or water feature. Chemicals are included. Request a quote and we'll give you an exact price after a free inspection.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. All of our plans are month to month. You can pause or cancel anytime with a quick call or text.

Do I need to be home during service?

No. Just make sure we can get through the gate and that pets are inside. We text you a photo when we're done.

How fast can you clean a green pool?

Most green pools are clear in 3 to 5 days, depending on how far the algae has spread and the condition of your filter.

Do you repair pool equipment?

Yes. Our certified techs repair and replace pumps, filters, heaters, salt systems and automation for all major brands.
