Our story

Started with one truck and one promise

Clearline Pool Care started in 2011 with one truck and one simple promise: show up on the same day every week and do the job right. Back then the biggest complaint we heard about pool companies was missed visits and rushed work.

Today we look after more than 2,400 pools across the East Valley, but the promise hasn't changed. Every customer gets the same technician every week, a photo report after every visit, and a team that answers the phone.
